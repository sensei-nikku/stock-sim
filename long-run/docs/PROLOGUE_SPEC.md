## Storyboard

### Practice Year 1 — MED · the emotional whipsaw (Nick's beats, verbatim intent)
- Q0 Onboarding: intro to the game, the buttons, and the first stock (MED, steady healthcare). No money yet.
- Q1 First paycheck: $100 arrives. Forced buy of MED right away.
- Q2 It slides, a ton, on a scary headline. Forced sell at a loss.
- Q3 It rose again but you already sold. Forced panic-buy, higher than you sold.
- Q4 It stabilizes a bit lower. You're learning: buy and hold into Year 2.
- Net: small loss (~ -$18).

### Practice Year 2 — TBK · you get paid to hold
- Q1 $100 in. Buy TBK. Q2 drifts up, hold. Q3 dividend lands. Q4 tempted to sell
  "since nothing's happening," you hold; it ticks up. Net: modest gain.

### Practice Year 3 — NVT · chasing the hype
- Q1 $100 in, buy NVT. Q2 hype spike, forced buy more at the top. Q3 it fades.
  Q4 settles below the spike, hold. Net: small loss.

### Practice Year 4 — PPK · the false rumor
- Q1 $100 in, buy PPK. Q2 rumor spike ("massive new oil reserve!"), forced buy.
  Q3 rumor FALSE, PPK craters. Q4 partial recovery, hold. Net: loss, partly recovered.

### Practice Year 5 — SHP · you've got it now
- Q1 $100 in, buy SHP. Q2 holiday bump; first year you are NOT forced into the
  mistake — you hold. Q3 normalizes. Q4 small dividend, steady finish. Net: gain.

Illustrative shape: Y1 -$18 · Y2 +$70 · Y3 -$45 · Y4 -$30 · Y5 +$223 ≈ +$200.

## The explanations (Nick, June: "only useful if it explains in detail the faults
made and what to look out for going forward")

Each beat's panel: (1) name the fault, (2) show the mechanism with the actual
numbers, (3) the rule to watch for going forward. Plain, adult-to-adult, no scolding.
Numbers below are June placeholders; final copy uses the back-solved prices.

- MED — sold the dip, chased the rebound. The company never changed; only the
  headline and your nerves did. The round trip took the money.
  Rule: did the business change, or just the headline?
- TBK — the income you'd have skipped. A dividend landed for doing nothing.
  Rule: boring can be good; "nothing's happening" isn't a reason to sell.
- NVT — bought the hype after the jump; good news was already priced in.
  Rule: already spiked + everyone excited = you're probably late.
- PPK — acted on a rumor that proved false.
  Rule: a rumor isn't a fact; wait for confirmation (the TRUE/FALSE tag).
- SHP — held through a normal seasonal bump.
  Rule: know the normal rhythm; don't flinch at the expected.

Closing recap card: the five rules above, framed as the lens for the real 15 years.
